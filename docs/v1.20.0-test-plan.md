# Cassette Motion Pro v1.20.0 test plan

## 1. First visual impression

1. Open Cassette Motion Pro on the normal fit-day laptop.
2. Open a client and fit session.
3. Confirm the workspace immediately shows the dark numbered workflow rail:
   **01 Fit Day**, **02 Client**, **03 Video**, **04 Measurements**, and
   **05 Report**.
4. Confirm the selected stage uses the lime highlight and inactive stages remain
   readable on the dark rail.
5. Confirm the active client, bike, session, and autosave state remain visible.

## 2. Laptop layout and video space

1. Test at the laptop's normal Windows display scaling and resolution.
2. Open every workflow stage and scroll to its bottom.
3. Confirm buttons, fields, and bottom actions are reachable without horizontal
   clipping.
4. Choose **← VIDEO** and confirm the fit panel collapses without closing the
   active client or session.
5. Show the Fit Panel again and confirm the workspace is restored at the same
   stage.
6. Confirm more of the video canvas remains visible beside the open fit panel.

## 3. Fit-day navigation

1. Move through all five numbered stages with the mouse.
2. Repeat using Ctrl+1 through Ctrl+5.
3. Confirm Client still exposes Setup, Files, and History choices.
4. Confirm Report still exposes Review and Builder choices.
5. Use F6 / Next Step and confirm it opens the expected stage.

## 4. Core Kinovea regression

1. Record a short Before clip and After clip.
2. Open both for analysis.
3. Confirm playback, drawing tools, favorite-frame saving, Save Image, and Save
   Video still work.
4. Confirm saved media goes into the active client's correct Before/After folder.
5. Complete one measurement and preview the report.

## 5. Persistent shell

1. Toggle **Hide Fit Header** and **Show Fit Header**.
2. Confirm the active session remains open through both actions.
3. Confirm **Do Next**, **Review**, **Show/Hide Fit Panel**, and the client picker
   remain readable and usable.
4. Close and reopen the workspace and confirm no stage or session data is lost.

v1.20.0 passes when the new shell is visually clear on the real laptop, every
stage remains reachable, more video stays visible, and all core Kinovea capture,
playback, drawing, and saving behavior remains intact.
