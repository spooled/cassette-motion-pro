# Cassette Motion Pro v1.9.0 test plan

This release makes bike measurements prove their calibration accuracy before the fitter accepts them.

## Camera setup

1. Open a client fit session and save a clear side-view image to Bike Before or Bike After.
2. Open Measurements and choose the matching Bike measurement action.
3. Confirm the calibrated workspace asks for a camera profile.
4. Select **NexiGo N980P · 120° ultra-wide** and confirm the setup checklist warns you to keep the bike near the image center.
5. Confirm N680P, standard 70–90°, and custom/unknown profiles are also available.

## Calibration and verification

1. Click **Calibrate Scale** and mark both ends of a known reference in the same plane as the bike.
2. Enter its real length in millimeters.
3. Click **Verify Calibration** and mark a different known distance, preferably farther across the image.
4. Enter the second real length.
5. Confirm the workspace shows the expected length, measured length, percentage error, and one of these results:
   - High at 1.0% error or less.
   - Moderate above 1.0% through 2.0%.
   - Review above 2.0%.
6. Intentionally enter an incorrect verification length and confirm the workspace recommends checking camera alignment, reference plane, and lens distortion.

## Bike landmarks and measurements

1. Optionally set a level reference using the floor or axle line.
2. Place the bottom bracket, saddle, grip/hood, and advanced bike landmarks.
3. Confirm the calculated measurements remain referenced to the bottom bracket and update when an orange landmark is moved.
4. Confirm the final save review includes the camera profile, calibration reference, verification result, quality warnings, and measurement values.
5. Save to Before, repeat for After, and confirm the correct columns are updated.
6. Physically measure saddle tip to the selected grip/hood contact point with a tape. Confirm the software's **Saddle tip to grip** is the straight-line distance and does not match the shorter horizontal-only distance when the bar is lower than the saddle.
7. In Advanced mode, choose **Rear edge → calculate center**, leave the diameter at 31.8 mm, and click the rear edge of the bar at the clamp. Confirm Handlebar X/Y, Reach, and Drop use the calculated center. Change the diameter and confirm the values recalculate.
8. Repeat on a bike facing the opposite direction and confirm the center offset still moves toward the front wheel.

## Session evidence and safety

1. Confirm the annotated evidence image is saved inside the active client's matching Bike Before or Bike After measurement folder.
2. Close and reopen the fit session; confirm the measurement values and camera/calibration summary remain in the session.
3. Confirm skipping verification is allowed only after a warning and marks the result **Skipped · review required**.
4. Confirm existing Kinovea capture, playback, drawing, Save Image, and Save Video tools still work.
5. Confirm Rider Measurements and report generation are unchanged.

## Pass condition

v1.9.0 passes when a fitter can select the camera profile, calibrate and independently verify scale, see the percentage error before saving, store Before/After bike measurements, and retain a clear accuracy trace in the client session.
