# Cassette Motion Pro v1.21.0 test plan

## 1. Consolidated laptop layout

1. Open Cassette Motion Pro at 1366 × 768 and 125% Windows scaling.
2. Open a client and fit session.
3. Confirm there is no duplicate black client/session banner inside or above the
   fit workspace.
4. Confirm the five workflow stages appear in one horizontal bar at the top.
5. Open every stage and confirm its bottom actions remain reachable by scrolling.

## 2. Video and workspace focus

1. Click **VIDEO FOCUS**.
2. Confirm the fit panel closes and the video canvas uses the available width.
3. Click **FIT WORKSPACE**.
4. Confirm the same client, session, and workflow stage return.
5. Confirm recording, playback, drawings, Save Image, and Save Video still work.

## 3. Remembered layout

1. Drag the thin divider at the left edge of the fit workspace.
2. Resize the client-session column using its divider.
3. Toggle the main fit header and choose either Video Focus or Fit Workspace.
4. Close and reopen Cassette Motion Pro.
5. Confirm the panel width, client-session column width, header state, and focus
   state are restored.

## 4. Stage-specific actions

1. Visit Fit Day, Client, Video, Measurements, and Report.
2. Confirm the bottom action row only shows actions useful to the active stage.
3. Confirm Report shows Save Draft, Review, and Preview.
4. Confirm non-report stages show Save and Next Step without report-only clutter.

v1.21.0 passes when the workspace remains readable on the fit-day laptop, the
video canvas can be restored with one click, layout choices survive a restart,
and no Kinovea capture, playback, drawing, or saving behavior regresses.
