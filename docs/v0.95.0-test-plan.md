# v0.95.0 Integrated Measurements and Evidence — Test Plan

## Open the integrated review

1. Open a client and an existing fit session.
2. Click **Review** in the persistent fit-day header.
3. Confirm the Fit Panel opens to **Integrated Review** inside the main Kinovea window.
4. Confirm the active client, session, and readiness count are correct.

## Measurements

1. Enter several Before and After bike or rider measurements.
2. Return to **Integrated Review** and click **Refresh Review**.
3. Confirm the completed-field count increases.
4. Click **Combined Measurements** and confirm the existing combined review opens.

## Favorite frames and saved images

1. Click **Favorite Frames** and save Before and After frame notes.
2. Click **Compare + Approve** and approve the comparison.
3. Confirm the integrated review shows both favorites and the approval state.
4. Select Before, After, side-by-side, or measurement-reference report images.
5. Confirm the selected-image count and filenames appear in the review.
6. Click **Saved Media** and confirm the active session media library opens.

## Fitter approval

1. Click **Review + Approve Report** and approve the current report content.
2. Confirm the approval section changes to approved.
3. Edit report-facing content and confirm approval returns to pending or needs renewal.

## Fit-day regression checks

1. Hide and reopen the Fit Panel; confirm the persistent header remains available.
2. Confirm **Do Next** still follows the guided workflow.
3. Confirm dual capture, playback, drawing tools, and Before/After/Dual saves still work.
4. Switch clients and confirm the integrated review shows only the newly active session.
5. Test at laptop resolution and confirm the review page scrolls and its buttons remain reachable.
6. Confirm the GitHub Actions Windows build passes and produces the portable bundle and installer.
