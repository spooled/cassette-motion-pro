# Cassette Motion Pro v1.7.0 test plan

This update makes the four real measurement choices visible immediately and separates their saved evidence clearly inside the active client session.

## Quick Start layout

1. Open a client fit session and select **Measurements**.
2. Confirm the top of Measurement Review shows four large choices: Before Bike, After Bike, Before Rider, and After Rider.
3. Confirm the existing tracking, correction, confidence, Bike Metrics, and Body Angles tools remain available below.
4. Resize the Fit Panel and confirm the Quick Start row scrolls rather than hiding actions.

## Before and After bike measurements

1. Save a clear Before image, then click **Before Bike measurements**.
2. Confirm the saved Before image opens automatically and **Save to Before** is the highlighted choice.
3. Complete calibration and landmarks, save, and confirm the values appear in the Before Bike Metrics column.
4. Repeat with **After Bike measurements** and confirm the values appear in the After column.
5. Confirm annotated images are saved under `Measurements/Bike/Before` and `Measurements/Bike/After` for the active session.

## Before and After rider measurements

1. Click **Before Rider measurements** and confirm the Before image opens automatically.
2. Correct the suggested rider points and save to Before.
3. Repeat for After and confirm values are stored in the correct Body Angles columns.
4. Confirm annotated images are saved under `Measurements/Rider/Before` and `Measurements/Rider/After` for the active session.

## Session safety

1. Click **Open Measurement Folders** and confirm it opens the active session—not another client.
2. Save, close, and reopen the session; confirm all four sets of values and evidence paths remain available.
3. Confirm report preview, Kinovea playback, drawing, and native measurement tools still work normally.

## Pass condition

v1.7.0 passes when all four measurement choices are visible without searching, each opens the correct saved image, values are stored in the intended Before/After columns, and annotated bike and rider evidence is separated into the correct active-session folders.
