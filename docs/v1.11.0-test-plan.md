# Cassette Motion Pro v1.11.0 test plan

> Version 1.11.1 stability check: untouched saddle height and setback must remain within the broad physical review ranges. The app must reject a transform that would produce extreme results such as a 2307 mm saddle height or 1900 mm setback.

## Dual-wheel perspective calibration

1. Open a client fit session and start Before Bike Measurements from a clear side-view image.
2. Confirm **Dual-wheel perspective** is selected (the first and recommended choice).
3. Enter the tape-measured wheelbase and unloaded outside tire diameter.
4. Click the eight requested points in order: rear axle, rear tire top, rear tire left/right at axle height, front axle, front tire top, front tire left/right at axle height.
5. Confirm the live magnifier follows the pointer and makes the axle and tire edges easier to place.
6. Confirm the screen draws both wheel crosses and the axle line.
7. Confirm calibration finishes with a residual in millimeters and a High, Moderate, or Review grade.
8. Confirm calibration points remain visible and locked while placing measurement landmarks.
9. Complete the landmarks without moving them to chase known values.
10. Compare untouched saddle height and saddle setback with independent tape/plumb-line measurements.

## Perspective and warnings

1. Repeat with the camera slightly off-axis and confirm the dual-wheel result is closer to the independent measurements than Quick Bike Reference.
2. Deliberately misplace a tire edge and confirm the residual increases or changes to Review.
3. Confirm the review shows an approximate tolerance rather than implying 0.1 mm accuracy.
4. Confirm a large residual produces a quality warning before saving.

## Fallback compatibility

1. Select **Quick bike reference** and confirm the four-point v1.10 workflow still works.
2. Select **Known reference / board** and confirm the original two-point calibration and independent verification still work.
3. Change wheelbase or tire diameter after calibration and confirm the calibration is cleared with instructions to run it again.
