# v1.23.0 Cleaner Measurement Cards and Approval — Test Plan

## Build and upgrade

- Confirm GitHub Actions produces the portable zip and installer.
- Open a client session created in v1.22.0 or earlier and confirm its measurements still load.
- Confirm Position History from v1.22.0 remains available.

## Measurement cards

1. Open a client fit session and select **Measurements → Measurement Review**.
2. Confirm the main review is shown as cards rather than a long text table.
3. Confirm each card shows Before, After, change, and one clear state.
4. Confirm required missing final bike values show **NEEDS VALUE** or **NEEDS FINAL**.
5. Confirm optional empty rider or bike values show **NOT RECORDED** without blocking the workflow.
6. Enter an unusually large change and confirm that card shows **REVIEW CHANGE**.
7. Enter a value outside a broad advisory range and confirm it shows **REVIEW**.
8. Choose **EDIT** on a bike card and a rider card; confirm each opens the correct editing screen.

## Approval

1. Select **APPROVE MEASUREMENTS**.
2. Confirm both fitter-review checkboxes are required before approval.
3. Approve and confirm the strip changes to **FITTER APPROVED**.
4. Edit any measurement and return to Measurement Review.
5. Confirm approval is automatically cleared and must be renewed.
6. Save, close, and reopen the session; confirm the current approval state persists.

## Laptop and workflow check

- Test at 1366×768 with the fit panel visible.
- Open Fit Day, Client, Video, Measurements, and Report; confirm each page heading and first line begin fully below the black stage bar.
- Confirm the black stage bar never covers page text when switching stages or resizing the window.
- Confirm cards scroll vertically and do not cover the bottom action bar.
- Confirm the quick Before/After bike and rider buttons remain reachable.
- Run **Quality Check** and confirm detailed warnings remain available.
- Preview the report and confirm approved measurement values are unchanged.
