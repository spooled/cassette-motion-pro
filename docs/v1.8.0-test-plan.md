# Cassette Motion Pro v1.8.0 test plan

This update makes bike and rider measurement destinations available directly from Kinovea's image and video save buttons.

## Save Image choices

1. Open a client fit session, open or capture a video, pause on a clear frame, and click **Save Image**.
2. Confirm the dialog shows Report images: Before, After, and Dual.
3. Confirm it also shows Measurement evidence: Bike Before, Bike After, Rider Before, and Rider After.
4. Save one image to each measurement destination.
5. Confirm the files appear under the active session's `Photos/Fit Sessions/<session>/Measurements/Bike/Before`, `Bike/After`, `Rider/Before`, and `Rider/After` folders.

## Measurement Quick Start

1. Save a distinctive image to **Bike Before**, then open Measurements and choose **Before Bike measurements**.
2. Confirm that image opens automatically without browsing.
3. Repeat with **Rider After** and **After Rider measurements**.
4. Confirm measurement images do not replace the main Before, After, or Dual report-image selections.

## Save Video choices

1. Export a video and click **Save Video**.
2. Confirm the same four measurement destinations are available.
3. Save a short test video to each destination.
4. Confirm the files appear under the active session's `Videos/Fit Sessions/<session>/Measurements/Bike/Before`, `Bike/After`, `Rider/Before`, and `Rider/After` folders.
5. Confirm measurement videos do not replace the main Before or After analysis videos.

## Safety checks

1. With no client fit session open, click Save Image and Save Video and confirm the app asks you to open or create a session.
2. Confirm Regular Save still opens the standard Kinovea save dialog.
3. Confirm the existing Before, After, and Dual image/video saves still work.
4. Confirm capture, playback, drawing, and measurement tools remain unchanged.

## Pass condition

v1.8.0 passes when images and videos can be routed directly to all four bike/rider measurement folders, guided measurements find the matching saved images automatically, and report Before/After media is not overwritten.
