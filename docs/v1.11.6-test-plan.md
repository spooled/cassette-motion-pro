# Cassette Motion Pro v1.11.6 test plan

## Tape-calibrated vertical scale

1. Open the reference bike image and complete Dual-wheel calibration with wheelbase 1050 mm and wheel preset 700.
2. Place the four basic landmarks. Before tape calibration, confirm saddle height is labeled **Wheelbase-only estimate**.
3. Enter the tape saddle height of 684 mm.
4. Click **Calibrate Vertical Scale**.
5. Confirm the vertical scale shows a locked correction close to 1.069× when the uncorrected result was 640 mm.
6. Confirm saddle height becomes 684.0 mm and the source says **Tape-calibrated vertical scale**.
7. Slightly drag the saddle-top point and confirm the value changes instead of remaining forced to 684 mm. Drag it back and confirm it returns near 684 mm.

## Reset and safety

1. Clear the landmarks and confirm vertical calibration returns to **not tape calibrated**.
2. Change wheelbase or calibration method and confirm the vertical correction resets.
3. Enter a value that would require a correction outside 0.75×–1.35× and confirm the app rejects it with point-placement guidance.

## Report and regression

1. Save the measurement and confirm its preview includes the tape-calibrated source and correction factor.
2. Confirm the 595 mm saddle-to-hood tape override still works.
3. Confirm capture, playback, drawing, Before/After saving, zooming, and panning are unchanged.
