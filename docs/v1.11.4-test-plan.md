# Cassette Motion Pro v1.11.4 test plan

## Hybrid saddle-height calculation

1. Open the same side-view bike image used for the v1.11.3 test.
2. Select **Dual-wheel perspective**, enter the tape-measured wheelbase and actual outside tire diameter, and complete all eight wheel points.
3. Place the bottom-bracket center and saddle-top landmarks without moving them to chase the known result.
4. Confirm the results show **Saddle-height source: Wheel-based vertical scale (perspective-safe)**.
5. Compare saddle height with the independent tape value. For the current reference bike, compare against 684 mm and record the difference.
6. Confirm saddle setback and wheelbase remain plausible and no previously saved measurement is changed.

## Tape-verified saddle-to-hood reach

1. Complete the saddle-tip and hood hand-contact landmarks.
2. Leave the tape option off and confirm the source says **Camera estimate**.
3. Enable **Use tape value when camera view is angled**, enter 595 mm, and confirm the displayed reach becomes 595.0 mm.
4. Confirm the source says **Tape verified (preferred report value)**.
5. Save to Before or After and confirm the saved preview/report keeps both the value and its source.

## Compatibility

1. Confirm Quick Bike Reference and Known Reference / Board still calculate measurements.
2. Confirm zooming, panning, dragging landmarks, Before/After saving, capture, playback, and drawing tools behave as before.
