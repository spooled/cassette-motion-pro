# Cassette Motion Pro v1.12.0 test plan

## 1. Reusable professional camera profiles

1. Open or create a client fit session.
2. Open **Video**, then the reusable dual-camera setup section.
3. Select **Professional USB 60 FPS**.
4. Confirm the profile shows 1920 × 1080, 60 FPS, manual/locked exposure,
   1/500 s shutter, DirectShow/UVC, and a lens-calibration reminder.
5. Change at least one field, choose **Save as Custom**, close the workspace,
   reopen it, and confirm the custom profile is still available.

## 2. Profile preflight

1. Select a professional profile and choose **Run Camera Check**.
2. Confirm the dialog separates profile checks from the physical-camera test.
3. Change frame rate to 30 FPS and exposure to Auto.
4. Run the check again and confirm both settings are flagged for review.
5. Restore 60 FPS and manual/locked exposure before recording.

## 3. Standard USB camera recording

1. Apply the profile and open **Record Before**.
2. Select the intended USB camera in Kinovea's capture controls.
3. In camera properties, select 1920 × 1080 at 60 FPS when supported.
4. Lock exposure/gain and set the shutter near 1/500 s, adding light if the
   image becomes too dark.
5. Record ten seconds of pedaling.
6. Play the saved file and confirm smooth motion, readable frame rate, no
   obvious dropped-frame jumps, no brightness pumping, and acceptable blur.
7. Confirm `Camera Setup.txt` in the session video folder records the selected
   profile settings.

## 4. IDS DirectShow validation

This step requires the physical IDS camera and must be completed on the fit-day
Windows computer.

1. Install IDS peak and include its DirectShow component.
2. Restart Cassette Motion Pro after installation.
3. Select **IDS 60 FPS DirectShow**, then choose **Record Before**.
4. Confirm the IDS camera appears in Kinovea's camera list and opens without a
   vendor-SDK error.
5. Select the supported 60 FPS format and record ten seconds of pedaling.
6. Repeat the playback checks above.
7. Close and reopen capture twice to confirm the device reconnects reliably.

If the camera does not appear, verify it in IDS peak first. Passing IDS peak but
not Kinovea indicates the DirectShow component or format selection needs review;
it does not indicate a client-folder or Cassette Motion Pro profile problem.

## 5. Lens and measurement check

1. Put a checkerboard or another rigid grid in the same plane as the bicycle.
2. Confirm straight lines do not visibly bow near the rider/bike measurement
   area. Avoid ultra-wide modes when possible.
3. Repeat one known bike measurement three times without moving the camera.
4. Confirm the result is repeatable within the studio's accepted tolerance.
5. Record the lens check in the profile, save the custom profile, and use that
   exact camera distance, resolution, and lens setting on fit day.

v1.12.0 passes when profiles persist, the preflight catches weak settings,
standard DirectShow capture remains unchanged, and the physical IDS camera
completes the dedicated IDS validation on the Windows fit computer.
